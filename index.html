What it can do

Real expressions
Parentheses, powers, factorials, modulo and implicit multiplication such as 2x or 3(4+1). Use ans to reuse the last result.
Your own functions
Type f(x)=x^2-4 once. Then use f(3) in the calculator or f(x) in any graph line.
Live graphs
Drag to pan, scroll to zoom and hover to read values. Points where a curve crosses zero are marked and listed.
Degrees or radians
One switch changes every trig function, including the inverse ones.
